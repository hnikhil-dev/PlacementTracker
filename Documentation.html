What is this Project in Simple Words?

If you explain this project to a college dean, a recruiter, or a first-year student with zero coding knowledge, here is what it does and why it was built.

Every year, college placement cells face chaos during campus recruitment drives:

The Problem Before

  • Students apply blindly: Students submit resumes to 50 companies without knowing if they actually match the requirements.
  • Unverified Resume Claims: Resumes say "Expert in Python, Docker, React", but recruiters find out the candidate only knows basics during the interview.
  • No Guidance on Missing Skills: When rejected, students have no idea what to study to qualify next time.
  • Placement Cells Drown in Excel: Training & Placement Officers (TPOs) manage hundreds of Google Sheets, manually matching students to jobs.

What This System Does

  • Automated Resume Scanning: Students upload their PDF resume. The system automatically reads it and populates their verified skills.
  • Skill Badges via Quizzes: Candidates take quick 5-question technical quizzes to earn verified badges. Companies can trust verified skills.
  • Real-Time Match Percentages: Every job posting shows a clear match score (e.g., "85% Match - Missing: Docker").
  • 30-Day AI Roadmap: With 1 click, AI builds a tailored 4-week learning calendar with actual YouTube & FreeCodeCamp links for missing skills.
  • Live Academic Leaderboard: Automatically ranks students based on CGPA, verified badges, shortlists, and placement offers.
The Two Main Portals

1. The Student Portal

Designed for undergraduate and postgraduate students to build verified profiles, discover tailored internships, practice technical quizzes, apply in one click, and track application statuses live.

2. The Placement Officer (Admin) Portal

Designed for College Placement Officers to post drives, review student submissions, verify official college academic records, give written feedback, and monitor real-time cohort rankings.

Key Takeaway: This is not just a job board. It is an intelligent feedback loop that helps students understand why they are not matching a job, shows them how to fix it in 30 days, and gives colleges verified student proof.

Complete Student User Journey

A clear, step-by-step walkthrough of how a student uses the platform from account creation to receiving placement offers.
  1. 1
    Create Account & Profile Setup
    Register on register.html using your student email. Enter your Department (e.g. CSE, IT, ECE), your Batch Year, and current CGPA. On login, you receive a secure token that keeps your session active for 7 days.
  2. 2
    Upload Resume PDF (Automated Skill Extraction)
    Go to your Profile tab and drop your resume in PDF format. The backend parser extracts your text and compares it against our master library of 160+ industry technologies (Python, React, AWS, Docker, etc.).
    Safe Cloud Storage: Resumes are securely saved in Supabase Storage. A direct link is added to your profile so recruiters and placement officers can view it with one click.
  3. 3
    Take 5-Question Quizzes to Earn Verified Badges
    Any student can write "Java" on a resume. To prove it, click the Verify button next to any skill on your dashboard.
    • You take a 5-question multiple choice technical quiz.
    • Score 4 out of 5 (80%) or higher to pass.
    • Passing awards you a permanent Verified Skill Badge that appears on your profile and increases your campus ranking.
  4. 4
    Review Opportunities & Match Ratios
    Open the Opportunities tab. The system compares your verified skills against every job requirement and calculates your match percentage:
    • 100% Match (Trophy Badge): You possess all required technical skills.
    • Partial Match (Star Badge): You have some skills, and missing skills are listed in red chips.
    Click Apply Now to submit your application immediately.
  5. 5
    Generate 30-Day AI Skill Gap Learning Plan
    If you don't match 100% of a job's requirements, click Gap Analysis on that job card. The AI compares what you know versus what the company wants and builds a 4-week learning calendar with real clickable links to YouTube and documentation.
  6. 6
    Track Status & Download Profile Card
    In the Applications tab, track your submissions live: Applied → Shortlisted → Offered. You can also view written notes from placement officers explaining why you were shortlisted or what to prepare for the technical round.

College Placement Officer (TPO) Guide

How Training & Placement Officers manage recruitment drives, verify student credentials, and identify top performers.

1. Post Recruitment Drives

Under Post Job, publish opportunities. Specify company name, role title, stipend, duration, work mode (Remote/Hybrid/Onsite), job type, and comma-separated technical skills.

2. Student Directory & Verification

Browse all registered students, view uploaded resumes, and verify CGPA against university ERP. Toggle Verify College Credentials to officially endorse students.

3. Application Review & Feedback

Under Applicants, view all submissions sorted by match percentage. Update candidate status to Shortlisted, Offered, or Rejected, with written feedback.

4. Student Ranking Leaderboard

A unified leaderboard automatically ranks candidates using our composite formula. Highlight top performers for premier hiring companies like Google and Microsoft.

The Fair Leaderboard Scoring Formula

Instead of ranking students purely by academic CGPA (which ignores real-world coding ability), the platform uses a composite formula calculated directly in SQL:

Total Ranking Score =
    (CGPA × 10) -- Academic consistency (up to 100 pts)
  + (Verified Skill Badges × 8) -- Proven technical knowledge
  + (Job Offers Won × 25) -- Final placement success
  + (Shortlists × 10) -- Interview qualification
  + (Total Applications × 2) -- Active career engagement
  + (College Verified ? 5 : 0) -- Official university endorsement

System Architecture & Technology Stack

Comprehensive engineering overview detailing frameworks, database design, algorithms, and security measures.

Component Technology Engineering Role
Backend Framework Python 3.11+ / FastAPI / Uvicorn High-speed asynchronous ASGI server serving REST APIs under /api/* and OpenAPI Swagger docs at /docs.

Database Schema & Tables
Table Name Key Columns Security (RLS) Description
public.users id (UUID), email, password_hash, role, cgpa, verified_skills Enabled (Service Role) Stores student and officer credentials. Enforces role IN ('student', 'admin') constraint.
public.skills id (BIGSERIAL), name Enabled (Public Read) Master catalog of 160+ industry technologies used for autocomplete and regex resume matching.
public.internships id (UUID), company_name, role_title, required_skills (text[]) Enabled (Public Read) Active placement and internship listings with deadline tracking and skill arrays.
public.applications id (UUID), user_id, internship_id, status, ai_reason Enabled (Service Role) Junction table connecting students to jobs with ON DELETE CASCADE triggers and status tracking.
public.quiz_attempts id (UUID), user_id, skill_name, score, passed Enabled (Service Role) Audit history of technical quiz attempts used to verify skills.
Security & Data Protection
  • BCrypt Password Hashing: Passwords are never stored in plain text. Hashed with 10 salt rounds before database insertion.
  • Stateless JWT Tokens: Sessions use HS256 signed JSON Web Tokens containing user ID, email, and role, expiring after 7 days.
  • SQL Injection Immunity: All database operations execute through parameterized psycopg2 queries (%s placeholders).
  • Supabase Row Level Security (RLS): Enabled across all public tables to prevent unauthorized direct queries through public PostgREST APIs.

Setup, Execution & Client FAQ

Instructions for zero-friction launch on any machine and answers to common client questions.
Running the Project (Fastest Method)

Windows One-Click (run.bat)

Simply double-click run.bat in the root folder. It handles everything automatically:

  1. Checks if Python 3.9+ is installed (downloads & installs it silently if missing).
  2. Creates an isolated virtual environment (.venv).
  3. Installs all required dependencies from backend/requirements.txt.
  4. Bootstraps database tables, default admin, and master skills automatically.
  5. Automatically opens your web browser to http://localhost:3000/index.html.

macOS / Linux (run.sh)

Open your terminal in the root folder and run:

chmod +x run.sh
./run.sh

Or use Docker with zero host dependencies: 

docker compose up --build

Setting up Supabase from Scratch (Step-by-Step)

If you or your client are launching on a brand new laptop with a fresh Supabase account, follow these 4 simple steps:

  1. 1
    Create a Free Project on Supabase
    Go to supabase.com, sign up or log in, and click New Project. Choose a project name (e.g. placement-tracker), set a secure Database Password (make sure to write this down!), and select your nearest region.
  2. 2
    Get Connection String & API Keys
    • Database URI: Go to Project Settings (⚙️) → Database → Connection string (URI tab). Copy the URI and replace [YOUR-PASSWORD] with your password.
    • API Credentials: Go to Project Settings (⚙️) → API. Copy your Project URL and anon key.
  3. 3
    Create the "resumes" Storage Bucket
    In the left sidebar, click Storage (📁) → New bucket. Name the bucket exactly resumes and toggle Public bucket: ON. This allows seamless student PDF resume uploads and recruiter downloads.
  4. 4
    Run the Project (Automatic Table & Skill Bootstrapping)
    Paste the credentials into .env and double-click run.bat. You don't even need to write SQL queries—the backend's self-bootstrapper connects automatically, builds all 6 tables, seeds 160+ master skills, creates the default admin, and populates sample company listings!
Frequently Asked Questions (Client FAQ)
Q1: What happens if the client inserts a brand new, empty Supabase database into .env?
The application has an Automatic Self-Bootstrapper (init_db.py). On startup, it detects if the database is fresh, automatically runs supabase_setup.sql to create all tables and indexes, seeds 160+ master skills, creates the default administrator, and seeds 5 initial internship listings (Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, TCS). Zero manual SQL required.
Q2: What happens if Google Gemini AI key is offline or expires?
The system has a built-in Emergency Offline Fallback Engine. It includes curated question banks for 15+ major technologies (Python, Java, React, SQL, Docker, C++, etc.). If the Gemini API key fails or has zero quota, quizzes continue to work seamlessly without crashing.
Q3: What if someone uploads a scanned image PDF instead of a text PDF?
The pypdf text extraction engine parses standard PDF text layers. For scanned PDFs (which are raw images), it safely uploads the resume to Supabase Storage and informs the student to select or confirm their skills manually if text cannot be extracted, ensuring zero crashes.
Q4: How do we deploy this to production (cloud hosting)?
You can push the repository to GitHub and deploy to Render.com or Railway.app in under 3 minutes:
• Build Command: pip install -r backend/requirements.txt
• Start Command: python backend/run.py
• The static frontend auto-detects the production domain dynamically, requiring zero URL code changes.
